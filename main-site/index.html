Loved by the community

    Questions? Answered

    Does a page cost anything?

    No. Making a page and keeping it is free, with no paid tiers.

    Do I need an email address?

    No. Your page is your account: its name, a password, and a second step such as an authenticator app or a passkey. Link Telegram as well if you want a way to reset a forgotten password.

    What if I lose my phone?

    Sign in with your password and one of the ten backup codes you saved when you set up your page, or with a code sent to your linked Telegram. Then set up your new phone from Security.

    Can my page go straight to one link?

    Yes. Turn on the redirect in your editor and visitors see where they are going for five seconds, with a chance to report it, and then go there. Your links are kept for when you turn it off.

    What happens if I do not sign in for a while?

    Pages need a sign-in at least once a year to stay live. A page nobody has signed in to for a year is hidden until its owner signs in again. Nothing is deleted, and if you linked Telegram you get a reminder a month before.

    What can I see about my visitors?

    Daily totals: visits, clicks, the countries visitors are in, which sites sent them and whether they used a phone, tablet or computer. Nothing that says who a visitor is is ever kept.

    Which links are allowed?

    Any web address starting with http or https, except shortened links (they hide where they go) and sites on the threat lists we check against. Anyone can report a page or link, and the GFTV team reviews every report.

    Who runs OneLink?

    Global Furry Television's Technology and Infrastructure Development Sub-Committee (TIDSC), from Singapore. Check the bar at the top of the page to see how to recognise an official GFTV site.
