How it works

  1. Claim your name

    Pick a name for your page and set a password. No email needed.

  2. Add your links

    Add as many links as you like, put them in order, and hide any for later.

  3. Share your QR

    Share your link anywhere, or show your QR code so people can scan it.

Kept safe

  • Two-step sign in

    Every page is locked with a password and an authenticator app or a passkey.

  • Ways back in

    Backup codes, and Telegram if you link it, get you back in if you lose your phone.

  • Reviewed reports

    Anyone can report a page or link, and the GFTV team reviews every report.

  • Only web links

    Links must start with http or https, and open in a new tab without passing your page on.
