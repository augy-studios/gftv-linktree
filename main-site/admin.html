Moderation

        Blocked page names

        Nobody can register these. A page already using one is not affected; take it down from Pages.

          Blocked domains

          Links to these domains, and any subdomain of them, cannot be added and stop showing within a minute.

            Threat lists

            Links are also checked against HaGeZi's threat intelligence, fake store and pop-up ad lists, and against Google Safe Browsing when its key is set. Links already on a page are checked again every day, and the lists are fetched again every two weeks.

            Flagged connections

            Connections that tried five harmful addresses today, and cannot save links until the day is up. Only a one-way hash of each address is kept, so each is shown by the start of it. Unflag one that was caught by mistake; to free its page as well, release the page under Pages.

              Banned connections

              A connection is banned when a second page is quarantined from it, or when an admin bans a flagged one. It cannot create or change pages until unbanned, but can still view pages, sign in and report. Many people can share one address (mobile networks, schools, event Wi-Fi), so lift a ban that catches someone else.

                Blocked patterns

                Regular expressions, matched without regard to case. For links, any address that matches is refused and stops showing within a minute; for page names, matching names cannot be registered.

                  Names that need approval

                  A page name that matches one of these patterns can be registered, but the page stays hidden until someone approves it under Pages, Awaiting approval.

                    Testimonials

                    Shown under "Loved by the community" on the home page, in this order. Only add what people actually said, with their permission. The section is hidden while none are visible.

                    Waiting for approval

                    Sent in by page owners, who agreed to it being shown with their page's name. Approve to put it on the home page.
